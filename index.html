Our Story

Where "K" Is For Knowledge

Long Island Canna Klub (L.I.C.K.) was founded with a simple mission: to educate, elevate, and empower our community through cannabis knowledge and trusted resources.

We believe that knowledge is the key to a positive cannabis experience. That's why we focus on education, community events, and evidence-based information to help you make informed decisions.

  • Evidence-based cannabis education
  • Community-first approach with regular events
  • Resources for responsible use and harm reduction
  • A welcoming space for all experience levels
